Before tailoring your CV

Complete the ten research checks in the opportunity record. Record dates, links and unknowns. Check the official sponsor register against the employer’s legal entity and assess whether the specific vacancy is likely to sponsor you. Employer licensing alone does not confirm role sponsorship.

Use your master CV as the evidence baseline and note any material gaps before tailoring. For roles outside London, add “Open to relocation” to the CV. Draft a cover letter for applications you intend to pursue.
